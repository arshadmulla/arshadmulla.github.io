About

Research focus

My work looks at how large language models can be made faster to run, better grounded in real knowledge, and easier to trust. That spans hardware-aware inference, retrieval architectures, and methods for evaluating what a model actually knows versus what it's making up.

Efficient Transformer inference

Benchmarking and mixed-precision optimization across CPU and accelerator backends.

Retrieval-augmented generation

Architectures that ground generation in structured, up-to-date knowledge sources.

LLM evaluation & detection

Methods for measuring reliability, and for telling human writing from machine writing.

Trustworthy generation

Watermarking and controllability techniques for safer, more accountable model outputs.
